Handscroll

Privacy Policy

Handscroll does not collect, store, or send any personal data.

Photos

  • Photos you pick are processed entirely on your iPhone. There is no upload step.
  • Photos are reached through the system photo picker, which gives the app only the photos you choose.
  • Saving asks for add-only Photos access, and only writes the finished files to your library.
  • Files you export are saved to your photo library or shared by you. They are never sent anywhere else.

Data

  • There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no network access.
  • Settings such as the accent colour and whether the gesture hints have been shown are stored in the app's own preferences on your device.
