# Privacy Policy

Handscroll does not collect, store, or send any personal data.

- Photos you pick are processed entirely on your device.
- The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no network access.
- Settings such as the accent colour and whether the gesture hints have been shown are stored in the app's own preferences on your device.
- Files you export are saved to your photo library or shared by you. They are never uploaded anywhere.
- Photos are reached only through the system photo picker, and only to save finished files to your library.
